Papa – Mere Superhero

Papa ke kandhe pe baithkar,
Dekha tha maine poora jahaan.
Unke bina ye zindagi,
Lagti hai adhoora sa samaan.

Unki daant mein chhupi hoti thi care,
Unke haathon mein sab problems ka repair.
Kabhi friend, kabhi guide ban jaate,
Bina kahe sab kuch samajh jaate.

Happy Father's Day, mere Papa,
Aap ho sabse khaas.
Aap jaisa na koi tha,
Na hoga, na hai aas-paas.
